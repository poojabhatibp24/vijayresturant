# 🍛 Vijay Restaurant Jodhpur (Blue City, Rajasthan)

> **Authentic Royal Sweets, Handcrafted Malai Roti, Mewa Cream & Safe Heritage Dining in Jodhpur**

Welcome to the official repository for the **Vijay Restaurant** landing page. This website is tailored specifically to introduce international tourists, travelers, and food lovers to the rare, royal culinary heritage of Jodhpur, Rajasthan, with a special emphasis on hygiene, safe drinking water, and traveler convenience across our 2 prime outlets.

---

## ✨ Signature Royal Specialties

* **👑 Jodhpuri Malai Roti**: Jodhpur's rarest dessert masterpiece. Thin, delicate sheets made purely from clotted whole milk cream simmered for hours, infused with Kashmiri saffron, cardamom syrup, crushed Iranian pistachios, and crowned with edible silver foil (*Chandi ka Varq*).
* **🍨 Chilled Mewa Cream**: Rich, wholesome clotted cream whipped with almonds, cashews, pistachios, honey, and saffron, served cold in traditional earthen clay *matkas*.
* **🥛 Makhaniya Lassi**: Creamy saffron yogurt smoothie topped with freshly churned white butter.
* **🥟 Jodhpuri Pyaaz Kachori & Mirchi Vada**: Crisp golden pastry stuffed with caramelized onions and mild Bhavnagri chili fritters.
* **🍲 Royal Dal Baati Churma Thali**: The complete royal Rajasthani banquet with five-lentil Panchmel dal and pure cow ghee.

## 🛡️ Foreign Traveler & Hygiene Commitment

* **100% RO & UV Filtered Water**: All drinks, cooking, chutneys, and ice are made with multi-stage purified water. Sealed bottled mineral water available.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Deployment

This project is built with lightweight, zero-dependency HTML5, modern Tailwind CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

### How to Run Locally:
```powershell
# Open directly in browser
Start-Process index.html

# Or run the built-in local server
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File server.ps1
```

### Enable Free Hosting via GitHub Pages:
1. Go to your repository **Settings** on GitHub.
2. Click **Pages** in the left sidebar.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
4. Your site will be live instantly!
