# 🍛 Vijay Restaurant Jodhpur (Sardarpura & Katla Bazar)

> **Authentic Royal Sweets, Handcrafted Malai Roti™, Prem Pyala™ & Pure Desi Ghee Dining in Jodhpur • Est. 1975**

Welcome to the official repository for **Vijay Restaurant Jodhpur**. Famous for its iconic multi-storey turquoise blue building in Sardarpura and its historic 1975 origin near Achal Nath Mandir in Katla Bazar, Vijay Restaurant is Jodhpur's recognized home for the legendary **Malai Roti™** and **Prem Pyala™ (Mewa Cream)**.

---

## ✨ Signature Royal Specialties

* **👑 Jodhpuri Malai Roti™**: Jodhpur's rarest dessert masterpiece. Impossibly delicate sheets created from clotted whole milk cream, slow-simmered with Kashmiri saffron, cardamom syrup, crushed Iranian pistachios, and crowned with pure edible silver foil (*Chandi ka Varq*).
* **🍨 Chilled Prem Pyala™ (Mewa Cream)**: Our trademark cold delicacy whipped with chopped almonds, roasted cashews, pistachios, saffron, and wild honey, served chilled in traditional earthen *matkas*.
* **🥟 Special Malai Samosa**: Crisp sweet novelty pastry filled with mawa, reduced cream, and cardamom glaze.
* **🍲 Shahi Govind Gatta & Chakki Ki Sabzi**: Authentic Marwari royal curries prepared in 100% pure desi ghee.
* **🥛 Makhaniya Lassi**: Creamy saffron yogurt smoothie crowned with freshly hand-churned white butter (*makhan*).
* **🌶️ Jodhpuri Pyaaz Kachori & Mirchi Vada**: Freshly fried golden onion kachoris and mild Bhavnagri chili fritters.
* **🍛 Royal Dal Baati Churma Thali**: The complete royal Rajasthani banquet with Panchmel dal, ghee baatis, and churma.
