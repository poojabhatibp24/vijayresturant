The Famous Jodhpuri Malai Roti

Contrary to its name, this is not bread. Malai Roti is an impossibly delicate sweet formed solely from thin, velvety layers of clotted milk cream that skim the surface of slow-simmered, saffron-infused cow's milk.

Each sheet is skillfully lifted with a silver needle, stacked delicately in rounds resembling flatbread, drizzled with aromatic cardamom syrup, garnished with hand-crushed Iranian pistachios, and crowned with real Chandi ka Varq (pure edible silver foil). It melts instantaneously on your tongue like chilled silk.

The Rich & Nutritious Mewa Cream

Mewa is the Hindi/Marwari term for royal dry fruits. Our Mewa Cream is a lavish bowl of dense, chilled artisanal clotted cream blended with crushed Kashmiri almonds, roasted cashews, green pistachios, charoli seeds, pure saffron, and a hint of wild honey.

Loved by travelers from around the world as an indulgent, wholesome alternative to Western gelato or custard. It is rich, naturally sustaining, and prepared strictly with farm-fresh whole milk.

We know that hygiene and water quality are the #1 priority when visiting India. Here is how Vijay Restaurant guarantees you a delicious, worry-free stay in Jodhpur.

Customizable Spice Levels

Afraid of excessive chili heat? Just tell our team "Mild" or "Non-Spicy"! We adjust spice blends on made-to-order dishes so you experience flavors and aromatics without burning heat.

Our savories, samosas, kachoris, and curries are cooked fresh in small batches throughout the day. No stale leftovers or overnight refrigeration.
